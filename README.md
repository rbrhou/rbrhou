### Interesting Repos to Check Out

#### Random Matrix Theory for Covariance Cleaning *(_in progress_)*
Cleaning noisy empirical correlation matrices with tools motivated from random matrix theory, following the texts of Bun, Bouchaud & Potters (2016), Ledoit, O., & Wolf, M. (2020), to build more robust covariance estimates for portfolio construction.

#### Nonlinear Physics and Dynamics Simulations 
This is based on a cross-listed physics course at the University of Toronto about Nonlinear Dynamical Systems with applications in physics I took with Professor Erich Poppitz. The course primarily follows the text "Nonlinear dynamics and chaos" by Steven Strogatz. The repository contains some interesting plots and simulations of bifurcations, limit cycles, and others.
